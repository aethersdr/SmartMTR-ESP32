# Not for distribution

AetherKnob's face, layout and meter behaviour are closely based on
**SmartMTR** by **Dariusz Socha** (https://smartmtr.net/), including
ballistics ported from AetherSDR's SmartMTR port.

Keep this repository local. Do not push it, publish it, or share builds of it
until Dariusz Socha has been asked about distribution and has agreed.
A pre-push hook in this clone refuses every push.
